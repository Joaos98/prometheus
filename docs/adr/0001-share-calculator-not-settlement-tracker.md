# Prometheus is a share calculator, not a settlement tracker

Prometheus records expenses and computes each member's Share and monthly leftover balance; it deliberately does not record who actually paid, transfers between members, or who owes whom. Money movement happens outside the app. Chosen over payment-awareness and full Splitwise-style settlement because the household's real need is visibility into shares and leftovers, not debt tracking — and payment-awareness can be added later without invalidating this model.
